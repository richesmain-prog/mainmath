What the numbers mean ▾

Net margin — profit divided by sale price. Under 20% on a spec build is thin.
Cash you need — the most money that has to leave your pocket at any point, interest included.
Max lot price — the highest offer that still hits your target margin. This is the negotiation number.
All-in $/sf — every cost divided by finished square feet. Compare it to the sale $/sf. That spread is the deal.
